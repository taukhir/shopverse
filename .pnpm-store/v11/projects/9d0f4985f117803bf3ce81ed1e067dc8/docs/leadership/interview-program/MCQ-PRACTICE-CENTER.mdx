## How To Use The Result

- Below 60%: return to the subject overview and rebuild fundamentals.
- 60–79%: review every missed explanation, then retry after at least one day.
- 80–89%: practise verbal follow-ups: internals, failure modes, diagnosis, and trade-offs.
- 90% or above: move to scenario questions and a timed mock; MCQ recall alone is not interview readiness.

The answer review is deliberately unavailable during the test. After submission, every question
becomes a collapsible review card containing the correct option, your selection, and the reasoning.
The aggregate result and topic outcomes are also recorded locally for the
[Interview Practice Dashboard](./INTERVIEW-PRACTICE-DASHBOARD.mdx); selected answers are not retained.
